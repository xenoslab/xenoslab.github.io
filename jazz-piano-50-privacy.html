Privacy Policy

How Jazz Piano 50 processes information for accounts, learning progress, and display.

1. Operator and scope

The operator and privacy contact for Jazz Piano 50 is Harang Kim, based in South Korea. Privacy and service enquiries: ryuharang@naver.com.

This policy covers on-device learning and account sync. App-store purchases and payment processing, and services opened through external links, are also subject to those services’ own policies.

Korean PIPA Article 30

2. Information and purposes

  • Account: email, account ID, signup, login and email-verification information, and authentication sessions are used for authentication, account maintenance, and password recovery. For email-and-password signup, Supabase Auth manages password hashes; plain-text passwords are not stored in the learning database.
  • Consent: the notice versions, choices, and timestamps submitted at signup are kept to document the request.
  • Learning: completed lessons, the current lesson and page, exercise result/retry/revealed-answer status, attempt counts, selected answers, and keyboard note identifiers are used to resume learning and sync progress across devices.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound and note-name labels are saved. Language choice stays on the device.
  • Support: contact details, messages, and information you choose to email are used to answer and handle your request. Do not send passwords or full card numbers.
  • Service connections: the authentication and database provider may process IP addresses, request times, errors, and authentication events for security and operations. The app does not include a separate advertising-tracking or behavioral-analytics SDK.
  • Font display: the app loads DM Sans and Noto Sans KR through Google Fonts. Opening an app screen may request fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com even without signing in. Google receives the IP address and request details such as the URL, browser information (User-Agent), and any referrer sent by the browser. Account email, passwords, and learning progress are not included in these font requests.

Only if selected · Google or Apple sign-in

When you choose Google or Apple sign-in, you use that account to sign in to Jazz Piano 50. This section applies when you select social sign-in.

Email, provider type, provider account ID, and basic authentication information such as email verification are received through Supabase Auth to identify and sign in the account. If you choose Apple’s Hide My Email, a relay address may be provided instead of your real email. Your Google or Apple password is not given to this app or its operator.

Depending on the basic-profile permissions you grant, the authentication service may also receive a name or profile image. The app does not use that information for lessons or marketing. Access to mail contents, contacts, or files is not requested.

The selected provider’s privacy notices and permission choices apply on its sign-in screen. If you do not choose social sign-in, no account information is received through that method; you may use an available email login or on-device learning without signing in. Deleting the app account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

3. Recording, advertising, and payments

The app does not record or upload your real piano performance through a microphone. On-screen keyboard answers are learning records, not audio recordings. Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising.

App purchases and payments made through the App Store or Google Play are handled by that store. The operator does not directly receive or store card numbers. If payment or analytics processing changes, this policy will be updated and any legally required notice or consent provided.

4. Device storage and cloud sync

Without signing in, progress stays in this device’s browser or app storage. Clearing storage or removing the app may erase local progress.

After sign-in, account learning records sync with Supabase. Sign in with the same Jazz Piano 50 account on another device to resume synced progress. Offline changes will not appear elsewhere until sync finishes after reconnection.

Authentication sessions and account-specific learning copies are stored on the device. Signing out clears the current login session but does not delete the account. Cached account progress may remain; clear app/site storage after using a shared device. The app does not use advertising-tracking cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

Account information, account-linked consent metadata, and learning records are kept while the account exists. Successful account deletion removes the live authentication account and its linked progress, plus that account’s session and learning copy on the requesting device.

Deletion from the live service is separate from backup and security-log expiration. Residual information is removed under the retention schedule applicable to the provider’s service and configuration. The Supabase documents below explain backup recovery windows and log processing. Live deletion does not mean immediate deletion from every backup.

Support emails and request records are used while needed to respond, verify, and handle the request, and are deleted once no longer needed for those purposes. Records needed to meet a legal preservation duty or handle an ongoing dispute are retained only to the extent required for that basis and purpose. The app keeps no independent payment or transaction ledger; store purchase records are governed by the store’s policy.

7. Request account and data deletion

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes the account and its cloud learning records. It cannot be undone. Signing out or removing the app does not delete your cloud account.

You can also request deletion without installing the app. Email ryuharang@naver.com, preferably from your registered address, with the subject “Jazz Piano 50 account deletion request” and the email of the account to delete. Do not send your password. We will verify ownership using only necessary information and inform you of the result.

Copies on other offline devices may not be erased remotely right away. Clear app/site storage on those devices too. Store-managed purchase records are not deleted with the app account. Account deletion does not cancel a purchase or request a refund.

Email an account deletion request

8. Your rights

You or a legally authorized representative may email ryuharang@naver.com to request access or a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. Portability, objection, and other rights apply where provided by law. Identity checks are limited to what is necessary, and responses follow applicable legal deadlines.

To withdraw international-processing consent, contact the same address or delete your account. You may continue on-device learning without signing in after cloud sync stops. If a legal restriction prevents a request, we will explain the reason and legal basis.

Account registration is for people aged 14 or older. Minors must obtain any guardian consent required where they live; legally authorized representatives may exercise applicable rights. If we learn that account information from a child under 14 has been collected, we will verify the matter through the contact above and take the necessary deletion steps.

9. Protection and policy changes

Encrypted connections, account-based access controls, and server-side protection of service secrets limit access. Protect your device and avoid sharing login details.

Changes to data, purposes, providers, international processing, or retention will be announced in the app and here with an effective date. Separate consent will be requested when legally required.

2. 어떤 정보를 왜 처리하나요?

  • 계정: 이메일, 계정 식별자, 가입·로그인·이메일 확인 관련 정보와 인증 세션을 회원 인증, 계정 유지 및 비밀번호 복구에 사용합니다. 이메일·비밀번호로 가입할 때 비밀번호는 Supabase Auth가 해시 형태로 관리하며 학습 데이터베이스에 평문 비밀번호를 저장하지 않습니다.
  • 동의 기록: 안내문 버전, 동의 여부와 시각 등 회원가입 시 제출한 동의 기록을 해당 요청 확인에 사용합니다.
  • 학습 기록: 완료한 레슨, 현재 레슨과 페이지, 문제의 정답·재시도·정답 확인 상태, 시도 횟수, 선택한 답과 건반 음 식별자를 저장하여 이어 학습하고 기기 간 진도를 동기화합니다.
  • 설정: 소리와 음 이름 표시 설정 등을 저장합니다. 언어 선택은 기기 안에 보관합니다.
  • 문의: 이메일로 보낸 연락처, 문의 내용 및 문제 해결을 위해 직접 제공한 자료를 답변과 요청 처리에 사용합니다. 비밀번호나 카드 전체 번호를 보내지 마세요.
  • 서비스 연결: 인증·데이터베이스 제공자는 서비스 보안과 운영에 필요한 IP 주소, 요청 시각, 오류·인증 이벤트 등 접속 기록을 처리할 수 있습니다. 앱에는 별도의 광고 추적·행동 분석 SDK를 넣지 않았습니다.
  • 글꼴 표시: 앱 화면은 Google Fonts에서 DM Sans와 Noto Sans KR을 불러옵니다. 화면을 열면 로그인 여부와 관계없이 fonts.googleapis.com 및 fonts.gstatic.com으로 글꼴 요청이 발생할 수 있어, Google에 IP 주소와 요청 URL, 브라우저 정보(User-Agent) 및 브라우저가 보내는 참조 페이지 등 요청 정보가 전달됩니다. 이 글꼴 요청에 계정 이메일·비밀번호·학습 진도를 넣지 않습니다.
